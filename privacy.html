Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers Second Gear, a private single-user tool that publishes the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account.

2. What we collect from visitors

Nothing. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers, and has no forms. No personal data is collected from anyone who visits it.

3. What the tool stores, and why

DataPurposeWhere it lives
TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens To publish videos to the operator's own account Locally, on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator
Basic profile information returned by TikTok (account identifier, username) To confirm which account is authorized Locally, same file
The operator's own videos and captions To publish them Locally, on the operator's own computer

No data about any other person is collected, because no other person uses the tool.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The only outbound transmission is to TikTok's API, and only for the purpose of publishing the operator's own content.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they are revoked. The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings → Security & login → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the local token file. Revoking access immediately ends the tool's ability to post.

6. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not offered to any user other than the operator.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change accordingly.
