Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers Meowposts, a publishing tool that creators use to publish their own videos to their own TikTok accounts.

2. What we collect from visitors

Nothing. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers, and has no forms. No personal data is collected from anyone who visits it.

The tool itself stores only what a creator authorises it to store, listed below.

3. What the tool stores, and why

DataPurposeWhere it lives
TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens To publish videos to the creator's own account On the server that runs the tool, readable only by the service operator. Never sent to a browser.
Basic profile information returned by TikTok (account identifier, username) To confirm which account is authorised before each upload Same location, same protection
The creator's own videos and captions To publish them, and deleted once published On the server that runs the tool; a copy stays in the creator's own queue

No data about anyone else is collected: not viewers, not followers, not other creators, and no analytics of any kind.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The only outbound transmission is to TikTok's API, and only for the purpose of publishing the creator's own content.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they are revoked. A creator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings → Security & login → Manage app permissions), which immediately ends the tool's ability to post. A creator can also ask for their stored data to be deleted by email, and it is removed.

6. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age TikTok requires.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change accordingly.
