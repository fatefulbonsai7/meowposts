Second Gear

A personal publishing tool.

Second Gear is a private, single-user tool. It connects to one TikTok account — the operator's own — through TikTok's official OAuth flow, and publishes original short videos that the operator has written and produced.

It does not collect data from visitors, does not serve other users, and does not post on anyone's behalf but the operator's.
