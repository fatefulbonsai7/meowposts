Meowposts

Publishing for creators who batch.

Meowposts takes the videos you have already written, voiced and edited, and publishes them to your own TikTok account on the schedule you set — with the caption, cover frame and privacy level you chose.

You connect your account through TikTok's official authorisation flow. The tool never sees your password, and it can only publish to an account that has explicitly granted it access.

How it works

  • Connect. Authorise your own TikTok account once, through TikTok's consent screen.
  • Queue. Add finished videos. Each one carries its own caption, cover frame and privacy level.
  • Confirm. Before anything is sent, the tool shows the account nickname, a preview of the video, and the privacy levels your account allows.
  • Publish. At the time you scheduled — or immediately, if you say so. You decide, every time.

What it will not do

It does not post anything you have not confirmed, does not read your audience, does not touch any account you have not authorised, and does not keep your videos after they are published.
